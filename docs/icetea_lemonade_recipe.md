# Iced Tea / Lemonade Recipe

For iced tea and lemonade, the same basic principle applies. You have a **base-syrup** and an **acid** (lemon juice or lime juice).

Always start with the base-syrup.

---

## Iced Tea

**Ingredients:**

- 5 liters of water *(Steven's note: 4 liters works well — gives a darker, more intense result)*
- 1 kilo of sugar *(original) — reduce by ~75%, so roughly 200–250g; Steven tried 200g and found it still quite sweet, suggests 150g as a better target*
- 3 Tbsp of "Solfrugt" tea — adds the fruitiness (from Føtex)
- 3 Tbsp of pure black tea — adds the depth and dryness (from Føtex)
- Freshly squeezed lemon juice (or bottled fresh juice — a little inferior, but works)

**Method:**

Boil 2 liters of water and put in the sugar and the tea. Leave it for 20 minutes. If you want to speed up the process, add a lot of ice cubes so the base-syrup goes back to room temperature or colder. Strain it. Now add the remaining water so the iced tea gets less intense. Now add lemon juice until you like the balance of sweetness and freshness.

Voilà!

---

### Steven's Notes (July 2026)

- **Teas used:** Teas from [Østerlandsk Tehus](https://www.osterlandsk.dk/) at Nørreport Station, Copenhagen — specifically **Admirality Tea** (for structure/depth) and **Skovsbær** (for berry aroma).
- **Sugar:** Used 200g — still quite sweet. Target is probably closer to **150g**.
- **Water:** Used 4 liters instead of 5 — result was darker/more intense.
- **Steeping time:** 20 minutes is too long. Suggestion:
  - **Berry tea (Skovsbær): 5–6 minutes**
  - **Black tea (Admirality): shorter, steeped separately** to avoid over-extraction and bitterness.
- **Overall:** A really nice combination — Admirality for structure, Skovsbær for berry aroma, fresh lemon for brightness.

## Lemonade

**Ingredients:**

- 5 liters of water
- 1 kilo of sugar
- The desired fruit, herbs, juice, berries (whatever you want your lemonade to taste like). For herbs you don't need much; for berries at least a kilo. *(One of Steven's favourites: blackberries picked at Amagerbanen)*
- Freshly squeezed lemon juice (or bottled fresh juice — a little inferior, but works)

**Method:**

Boil 2 liters of water and put in the sugar and the berries/herbs/… Let it sit for at least 24 hours for optimal flavour. Stir often to crush berries. Because of letting it sit for 24 hours, it should be back at room temp or colder. Strain it. Now add the remaining water. Now add lemon juice until you like the balance of sweetness and freshness.

Voilà!
